# LittleLauncher/Controls

Reusable XAML views, panels and attached behaviors live here.

Read [xaml.md](../../docs/xaml.md) before changing these controls or their template wiring.
Read [components.md](../../docs/components.md) for component and namespace ownership.

`ConditionalNameToolTip` owns conditional full-name tooltips for launcher item templates and trimmed group/column captions. Template roots target the nearest list/grid item; attaching Text directly to a TextBlock uses that label as both trim source and tooltip target, keeping group tooltips away from child items. Keep state local, detach label subscriptions on unload, and read actual trimming without measuring or changing layout.

`LauncherItemsView.xaml` and its focused partials own the shared launcher renderer, editing, toolbar and custom drag/drop. Read [drag-drop.md](../../docs/drag-drop.md) for geometry, source-of-truth and async session rules, and [icons.md](../../docs/icons.md) for item/icon invalidation. `ILauncherItemsHost` separates window placement, modal HWND ownership and completion policy. Keep the same implementation in both native popup and regular editor hosts; no live visual-tree transfers, duplicate templates, reflow or new persisted geometry.

`WrappingPanel` follows the sibling custom-Panel measure/arrange convention for natural-width control rows. It keeps child/tab order and wraps the regular editor's three add actions in a finite Star column. Launcher settings is an independent icon button in an Auto column, aligned to the top right with the shared localized tooltip and accessible name. The regular host stretches the full toolbar row and supplies its viewport width; do not put settings back into the wrapping action collection. Native editing retains its five compact fixed slots and Done. Do not reuse the fixed-cell launcher item panel or change item geometry to lay out window actions.


`LauncherItemsView.Hover.cs` owns passive leading/trailing or corner grip-and-pencil cues for both editor hosts. Rounded translucent theme scrims and accent glyphs replace per-cue boxes; high contrast keeps opaque window/text colors. A separate outline marks the entire hovered real group while cues stay on the header; child hover takes priority. List/header text uses temporary native ellipsis within unchanged dimensions, restoring the exact local Margin on exit/recycle/unload/rebuild/close. Keep cues clipped, non-hit-testable and dimension-neutral; the whole item stays clickable/draggable. Entry and movement share innermost-container resolution to preserve child priority and recover child-to-header transitions. All hover handlers leave pointer Handled unchanged: WinUI can reuse enter state for a press, and handling the enter can suppress native drag capture. Root exits hide only outside the root.

`LauncherItemReorder.cs` owns window-free item move validation and column flattening, with regression cases in `tests/LauncherItemReorder.Tests`. Validate current source/target collection identity and exact item references before changing membership. Rebuild/cancellation invalidates internal drag tokens. Whole-group drags over child bodies route to the enclosing column for before/after reorder, never nesting. Insertion carets are overlays and must not change container geometry. `LauncherItemsView.DragPreview.cs` provides the bounded name/group-count card through in-view DragUIOverride while preserving native source gestures and cancellation cleanup. Read [drag-drop.md](../../docs/drag-drop.md) before changing either file.

`LauncherItemsView.DropTargets.cs` resolves the same destination for DragOver and Drop, including external payloads. `LauncherDropGeometry.cs` reserves up to eight DIPs at each real group's outer top/bottom for before/after sibling insertion; its middle and header accept children. Whole-group drags retain midpoint reordering, while synthetic wrappers have no visible group zones. Into-group feedback tints the whole receiver and uses an inset child caret; sibling feedback has no tint and spans the full outer group boundary with an explicit before/after caption. Keep both cues passive and clear them together on leave/rejection/drop/cancellation/rebuild/disposal. The pure runner covers exact thresholds, invalid geometry and distinct saved topology; native cases exercise actual nested routing and visual feedback.

At the end of a nonempty real group, progressively fade only the last child toward the insertion edge with the receiving overlay's theme gradient, keeping most content clear, and draw the inset horizontal marker within that child's lower area. Do not modify the child's Opacity, local layout values or input: it may itself be the native drag source. Draw the fill behind the gradient and the transparent full-group outline above it, with the inset caret in the higher RootGrid plane. The high-contrast scrim is transparent so text retains contrast; the inset marker, system outline and explicit caption distinguish membership. Outside/after feedback removes the fade and draws the full-width marker beyond the normal group bottom. No expansion slot, placeholder item or drag-driven reflow is used. Keep end-marker geometry in LauncherDropGeometry and derive scrim coordinates from the already-arranged group, not the overlay's pending Margin.

The three real-group templates share `GroupHeaderStyle`: transparent Background and 8,4,8,4 Padding preserve the old margin/text geometry while making the entire passive pencil area part of the real header hit target. Do not add overlay buttons or handle pointer enter to fix clicks. Preview dimensions follow the active mode; the current destination hint is a separate row above the identity card in one bitmap because native captions cannot be positioned. Cache by caption and mode, bound render/deferral waits, and guard application and native fallback by the current drag token and feedback version. Rejection must replace allowed feedback with Cannot move here.


`LauncherItemsView.Platform.cs` owns editor container visibility. Follow [drag-drop.md](../../docs/drag-drop.md) for its full-source storage boundary and [user-settings.md](../../docs/user-settings.md) for OS metadata. Normal-use group projections are presentation-only and may never be flattened into persisted items.
