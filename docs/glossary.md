# Little Launcher

Little Launcher provides named launchers for shortcuts and websites. These terms distinguish the launcher, its contents, and the ways users open or exchange it.

## Language

**Launcher**:
A named collection of shortcuts or a web browsing destination, with its own identity and opening behavior.

**Shortcut launcher**:
A launcher containing application or website shortcuts, optionally organized with headings, groups, and columns.

**Web launcher**:
A launcher containing a web browsing experience and its bookmarks rather than a collection of shortcut items.

**Flyout**:
The popup surface opened for a launcher, containing its shortcuts or web browsing experience.

**Global sync**:
Synchronization of the user's full collection of launchers between machines.

**Shared launcher**:
A launcher whose items are published for other users to subscribe to, separate from global sync of the owner's collection.

**Launcher platform**:
The operating system owning an entire shortcut launcher. Untagged legacy launchers belong to Windows; web launchers are shared. Items are never filtered by operating system.

**Foreign launcher**:
A shortcut launcher belonging to the other operating system, retained in Settings and sync as disabled, without a tray icon or running resources.

**Keep running**:
A web launcher's background policy that retains activity while its flyout is hidden, including startup in the background with Little Launcher.
