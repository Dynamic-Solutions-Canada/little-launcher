- Little Launcher now speaks nine languages: English (United States and Canada), Spanish, French (France and Canada), German, Simplified Chinese, Brazilian Portuguese and Japanese. It follows your device's language, or you can pick one under Settings, Language.
- Settings has a cleaner, more consistent look.
- New Reset settings returns your preferences to their defaults and keeps your launchers, sync and browser extensions.
- Website icons now come only from the website itself. Little Launcher no longer asks Google's icon service when a site has no icon.
- New one-way OneDrive shares now use a view-only link, so people you share with can't change your launcher. Links you created before stay as they were.
- The tray menu now reads Settings, and adds Open logs folder.
- Screen readers now announce every button and field by name.
- Fixed: new installs no longer start with SFTP sync turned on and nothing to sync to.
- Fixed: tray icons now come back after Windows Explorer restarts, and stay after an update.
- Fixed: Exit and restarting after importing settings now close the app reliably.
